The brief

Make the connection between entertainment partnerships, product context, and community participation legible. The work included co-branded campaigns with film studios and Web3 partners, plus listing-day GTM around AMAs, trading competitions, and social sprints.

The campaign system

Position the ecosystem, turn the position into a narrative, use partners to widen the invitation, and give the community a concrete activation to join. The feedback loop is the next useful move — not a decorative afterthought.
