The brief

Make collaboration useful across Web3 and Web2 audiences for a sports and lifestyle ecosystem. The work included a partnership framework, creator and KOL pipelines, and joint campaigns across partners.

The campaign system

Position the ecosystem around a shared cultural interest, identify partners who can make the invitation credible, then give creators and communities a clear way to participate. Keep the loop open so partner response improves the next campaign.
